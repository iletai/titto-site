Titto ✨

The Ditto-killer clipboard manager for Apple Silicon.

Current version: 0.8.8

🪟 Detached panel

Tear the history panel out of the menubar into a free-floating, resizable window. Per-display position memory and an optional always-on-top mode.

⌨️ Quick-copy ⌃⌥⌘1..9

Nine global hotkeys map to your nine most-recent clips. Sub-50 ms hotkey-to-paste end-to-end. Off by default; opt in per slot.

🛡️ Sensitive smart-skip

Likely passwords, tokens, and credit-card numbers are filtered out of search and quick-copy before they ever leave the database row.

Why Titto

  • Native arm64. No Electron, no JavaScript runtime.
  • FTS5 + BM25 + recency boost search, built for 100k+ clips.
  • Vietnamese-aware out of the box — type tieng viet, find "tiếng Việt".
  • Privacy-first: no analytics, no telemetry, no account, no server. Everything stays on your Mac.
